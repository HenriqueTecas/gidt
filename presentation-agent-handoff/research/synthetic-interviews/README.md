# Saved AI role-play transcripts

These seven files were recovered from the interrupted session and copied unchanged from `.context/synthetic/`. They contain **fictional dialogue generated by AI**, not interviews with people. The accompanying guide is the original prompt structure, not evidence that fieldwork took place.

Names, biographies, quotations, anecdotes, locations of interviews, elapsed times, consent statements, recordings, recruitment stories, tone and body language are part of the fiction. No recording or human consent record accompanies this material. Statements about named services, local streets, costs, eligibility or transport rules have not been verified through these scenarios.
