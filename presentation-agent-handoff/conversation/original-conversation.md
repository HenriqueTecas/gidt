## What an HMW is
An HMW is a **"How Might We…" question**. It turns an insight (a problem you found in your research) into an open question that leaves room for many possible solutions. It's the step between research and brainstorming. The Week 7 deck explains the three words:
- **How:** you don't know the answer yet.
- **Might:** there are several possible answers, not just one.
- **We:** the team works it out together.

The rules in the slides:
- Start from an insight.
- Write several, each covering one part of the problem.
- Never build a solution into the question.
- Make it neither too broad nor too narrow.

Two examples from your slides:
- The problem "teenage girls need to eat nutritious food" becomes "HMW make healthy eating something teenage girls aspire to?" and "HMW make nutritious food more affordable?"
- The insight "millennials want life to be an adventure, even in small everyday doses" becomes "HMW help companies make everyday work more fun and engaging for millennials?"

Your challenge, "How can we encourage youth… to participate and intervene in urban mobility solutions?", is already a very broad HMW. The mid-term wants sharper ones based on what you found, which is why the brief says "Reframed problem (HMWs)". Made-up examples:
- ❌ "HMW get teens to participate in mobility?" just repeats the challenge.
- ❌ "HMW build an app where teens vote on bike lanes?" has a solution built in.
- ✅ "HMW make teens feel their opinion on how they get around the city actually changes something?"
