THE COURSE LANGUAGE TO APPLY
- Double Diamond: Discover, Define, Develop, Deliver. The current work is
  in Discover/Define, connecting interview research to the reframed problem.
- Interview evidence is different from interpretation and insight.
- An insight explains a possible underlying motivation or cause; a quote,
  statistic, request or proposed solution is not automatically an insight.
- Point of view: [User] needs [Need as a verb] because [Insight].
- HMW: “How Might We…”, an open question derived from an insight. Do not
  build an app, budget, game or workshop into the question itself.
- User-centred scope: specific enough to investigate, broad enough to allow
  more than one possible response.
- Tools in the source materials include interviews, observation, extreme
  users, stakeholder maps, empathy maps, personas, journey mapping,
  documentary research and grouping observations into themes. Only claim
  methods actually used: desk research, seven semi-structured interviews
  and thematic synthesis.
