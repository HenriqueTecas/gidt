   Methodology (Experiment)
   The session encompassed three key exercises corresponding to the different phases of design thinking:



                               INSPIRATION
                                                           Each small group was asked to identify trends that they believed would impact their challenge in the future, creating
                                                           spaces of opportunity for value creation. After each small group discussion, each group submitted an identified
                               1. Identifying Trends       opportunity space in a brief and simple sentence.




                               IDEATION
                                                           In small groups, participants generated ideas that responded to the most voted opportunity space from the previous
                                                           exercise. Each group then selected one idea they considered to have the greatest potential for implementation and
                               2. Brainstorming            value creation and submitted it in a brief sentence.




                               IMPLEMENTATION

                                                           Each small group presented their final idea in a three minute pitch. The remaining groups provided feedback with one
                               3. Feedback                 positive point and one point for improvement. Each group then selected the feedback received they found most useful.

   Methodology (Experiment)

                           Voting Process                                                             Company Selection Rationale



         • After each 30-minute exercise, each small group submitted their results.
                                                                                           Expertise in blending AI with human skills and CX analytics.

         • Participants then voted individually on the best output, excluding their own
         group's submission. This voting approach reflects the "wisdom of the crowd"
         concept, where collective group decisions are often more accurate than those of
         individual experts (Surowiecki, 2005).
                                                                                           Emphasis on user-centric innovation aligns with design thinking principles.
         • In addition, leadership present in the session also voted on the best outputs
         from all groups despite not having carried out the exercises. Leaders voted
         without knowing which group produced each output, allowing for an unbiased
         comparison between participant (wisdom of the crowd) votes and leadership
         decisions.                                                                        Diverse participant selection from different departments fosters innovation
                                                                                           through varied perspectives (Thoring et al., 2014).

   Conclusions
         RESEARCH QUESTION


         How does the integration of generative artificial intelligence affect the traditional stages of the design thinking process, including inspiration, ideation, and
         implementation, within corporate environments?



         Generative AI greatly improves the traditional phases of the design thinking process. It increases creative confidence during the inspiration phase, enhances idea generation during the ideation
         phase, and improves receptivity to technological advancements during the implementation phase. Generative AI boosts creative confidence, helps in trend identification, encourages idea
         generation, and improves feedback receptivity, although it does not significantly affect ethical considerations.




         RESEARCH QUESTION



         What distinctive contribution does generative artificial intelligence bring to the specific stages of a design thinking process?




         In the inspiration phase, generative AI helps identify future needs; in the ideation phase, it stimulates a broad range of novel ideas; and in the implementation phase, it facilitates the exchange
         of feedback. There are the unique contributions of generative AI to each phase. At every stage of the design thinking process, generative AI provides distinctive advantages that promote
         innovation.
