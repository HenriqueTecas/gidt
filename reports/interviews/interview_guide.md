# Interview guide (semi-structured, ~25 min) — youth (14–18) & urban mobility in Lisbon

Intro: "We're students at Técnico doing a project on how teenagers get around Lisbon and whether they have a say in it. No right or wrong answers."

A. Warm-up / day in the life
1. Walk me through yesterday: how did you get to school and back? Who decided how you'd go?
2. What's the best and worst part of that trip?
3. Where else do you go during the week (sport, friends, weekends)? How?

B. Problems, workarounds, feelings
4. Tell me about a moment on your way somewhere when you felt unsafe, annoyed or stuck.
5. Is there anything you do to "hack" or work around a problem (routes, apps, timing, friends)?
6. What do your parents think about you moving around alone? Has that changed?

C. Voice and participation
7. When you notice something wrong (dangerous crossing, late bus, broken pavement), what do you do?
8. Has anyone (school, Câmara, Junta, Carris) ever asked your opinion about streets or transport? What happened after?
9. Do you take part in anything outside class — clubs, associations, causes, online communities? Why that one?
10. Have you heard of the Youth Participatory Budget (OP Jovem) or the Municipal Youth Council? Thoughts?

D. Scenarios ("what if")
11. Imagine your year had €5,000 and six weeks to change one thing outside your school. Would you want to be involved? How?
12. What would make you believe the Câmara would actually build the winning idea?
13. Would you rather join a youth council meeting or a 4-week team challenge with friends? Why?
14. If you could test a change on your street for one afternoon, what would you test?

E. Close
15. If you could tell the Mayor one thing about being a teenager getting around Lisbon, what would it be?
16. Anything we should have asked?
